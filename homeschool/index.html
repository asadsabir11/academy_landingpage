Frequently asked questions

Good to know before you start

Do I need to enroll in a school to take lessons?

No. Students can take tuition while studying at home, attending an online school, or preparing independently as private candidates.

Does The Digital Tutor register students for exams?

No. Tuition and exam entry are separate. Students or parents arrange exam registration directly with the British Council or another approved exam centre.

Can every Cambridge or Edexcel subject be taken as a private candidate?

Not always. Availability can depend on the exact syllabus and assessment components. Confirm the subject and options with the exam centre before entering.

How do you decide which class is right?

We discuss the student’s exam board, qualification, subject, syllabus, exam session, current progress, and learning goals before recommending group or one-to-one tuition.

Who teaches the classes?

Dr. Ibrahim Malick teaches the classes offered through this page personally.

What are the fees?

Group classes are PKR 30,000 per subject per month. One-to-one tuition is PKR 6,000 per hour. Contact us to confirm schedules and current availability.
