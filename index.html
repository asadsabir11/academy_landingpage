Who teaches the classes?

Classes advertised on this page are taught directly by Dr. Ibrahim Malick, PhD.

How much are Group Classes?

Group tuition is PKR 10,000 per subject per month.

Private 1-to-1 tuition is PKR 3,000 per hour.

Should I choose Group or Private tuition?

Group classes are best for students who want structured ongoing syllabus and exam preparation. Private tuition is best for targeted weaknesses, individual pacing, intensive exam preparation or specific problem areas.

Can I watch a lesson before enrolling?

Yes. Full demo lessons for Computer Science, Mathematics and English are available directly on this page.

Are both O Level and A Level students accepted?

Yes, depending on the subject and current availability.

Can Private Tuition focus only on weak topics?

Yes. Private sessions can focus on specific topics, past papers, exam technique or areas where the student needs additional support.

Is Physics available?

Yes. Group and Private Physics tuition is available. A sample lesson can be requested through WhatsApp.
