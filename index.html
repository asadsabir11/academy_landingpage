The teaching philosophy is simple: students remember more when they understand why something works. Lessons therefore start with the mental model, build the technical language, and then move into examination-style application.

For private tuition, the same approach becomes highly targeted: identify the weak area, diagnose the misconception, rebuild the concept and practise until the student can apply it independently.

Do you teach both O Level and A Level?

Yes. Use the WhatsApp enquiry to specify the student's level and subject so the relevant class information can be shared.

What is the difference between group and private tuition?

Group classes follow a structured class pathway. Private tuition is more customised and is better suited to targeted weak areas, individual pacing or intensive exam preparation.

How do I get fees and the current schedule?

Send a WhatsApp message with the student's level, subject and whether you prefer group or private tuition. You will receive the relevant current details.

What if my child is weak in only one topic?

Private 1-to-1 tuition can be used for focused support in a specific area, such as pseudocode and trace tables in Computer Science or a particular Mathematics topic.
